# Design System Documentation

## Theme Overview
This document outlines the core design tokens and guidelines for our digital products. The theme is configured for a **dark** color mode, providing a modern and comfortable viewing experience in low-light environments.

## Color Palette
The brand's color palette is defined with a vibrant approach, centered around a seed color that radiates warmth and energy.
- **Primary Color**: `#fe7104` - A bright, energetic orange, serving as the brand's most distinctive chromatic color. It is ideal for primary calls-to-action, important interactive elements, and key brand highlights.
- **Secondary Color**: `#c66903` - A deeper, rich orange, offering support for less prominent UI elements, such as secondary buttons, chips, and dividing lines.
- **Tertiary Color**: `#e6a505` - A golden yellow accent color, used for highlights, badges, informational alerts, or decorative elements that require an additional pop.
- **Neutral Color**: `#9e6c46` - A warm, earthy brown neutral base color, providing a foundational tone for backgrounds, surfaces, and non-chromatic UI components.

## Typography
Our typography system is designed for readability and aesthetic appeal across all interfaces.
- **Headlines**: `epilogue` - Used for all major headings and titles, providing a distinct and impactful presence.
- **Body Text**: `inter` - Employed for general body text, paragraphs, and longer content, ensuring legibility and comfort.
- **Labels**: `inter` - Applied to form labels, buttons, and other interactive elements, maintaining consistency with body text for clarity.

## Shape and Form
The geometric characteristics of our UI elements contribute to the overall feel of the design.
- **Roundedness**: `1` - UI elements feature subtle rounded corners, offering a soft yet precise aesthetic. This level of roundedness is enough to soften sharp edges without becoming overly circular.

## Spacing
The spacing system dictates the amount of whitespace, influencing density and readability.
- **Spacing**: `2` - A normal level of spacing is applied throughout the UI, striking a balance between compactness and an open, airy feel. This ensures elements have enough room to breathe without feeling sparse.
